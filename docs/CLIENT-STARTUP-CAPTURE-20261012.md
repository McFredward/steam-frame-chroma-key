# Successful native client-startup capture

The coordinated Frame PC-VR-stream run confirms the corrected startup path on
the actual Steam-runtime receiver. The prepared observer authenticated the
native mappings, captured the intended resource-manager pipe's successful
mutex initialization, and observed the original public OpenVR initialization
complete. This is a local object-birth result, not a connected socket namespace,
video-frame association or working passthrough.

## Recovery and limits

After separate maintainer rollback readiness, the reviewed session manager
removed its exact user-service override and restarted Steam in its original
configuration. Original service/native-file snapshots matched. An independent
scan inspected 57 readable user-owned process mappings and found no diagnostic
library. Four mapping-restricted systemd/PAM/Gamescope/SSH processes were outside
the changed Steam service scope; no inspection of all kernel processes is claimed.

All owned diagnostic/input files, the two temporary directories and the
runtime override were removed after their receipts were archived. Native
Steam/game files, system images, shaders and scene pixels were not modified.
The maintainer was told the Frame could enter standby. Private source/capture,
ownership, recovery and file-inventory evidence remains under ignored
`build/private-client-startup-instance-fixed-run-20261012/`.

The next adapter must establish the target's continuous connection lifetime
and the exact import/request/reply/reference transaction. Kernel endpoint
pairing, resource generation/retirement, selected video content, app-owned
coverage, reprojection/foveation UV and GPU completion are separate remaining
gates. Additional protected FD/PID or protocol-buffer reads require a new
reviewed runtime scope; the five startup-stack words do not authorize them.
The PC Steam sender remains unchanged.
